## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno’s job is to separate the signal from the noise and recommend what the product team should do next.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

User interview
Support ticket
CEO email
Sales call notes
Engineering Slack thread

**How fresh must it be**

Juno refreshes source data before making a recommendation and flags information that may be stale. Continue if sufficient context exists, but clearly identify what's missing and how it could affect the recommendation.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

Read and analyze multiple sources of information.
Synthesize scattered signals into a single picture.
Identify patterns and themes across customer, sales, executive, and engineering input.
Prioritize issues using RocketShip's P0–P3 framework.
Cross-reference constraints, such as roadmap commitments and engineering capacity.
Identify conflicts or unsupported requests, such as Sales promising functionality that doesn't exist.
Evaluate tradeoffs between customer impact, business value, strategy, and feasibility.
Recommend what Product should do next.

**Deliberate omissions**

Juno can recommend priorities and draft tickets, but it cannot change the backlog or roadmap without human approval.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Iterate until Juno has enough evidence to make a confident, context-aware recommendation, rather than repeatedly trying a fixed number of times.

**Latency and cost target**

Initial answer in <10 seconds most of the time; cap standard tasks at 3 loops. Allow an additional loop only when important context is missing or conflicting.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Last 30 days of information gathered

**Expiry and write rules**

Time-sensitive facts expire quickly and are refreshed from their source. Strategic information has a longer lifespan but is reviewed periodically. Human-confirmed corrections always override stored model output, and Juno should record the correction and its timestamp rather than continuing to rely on the old answer.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

There is no verification at this point. The data is created from information gathered but not verified

**Failure behaviour**

If a check fails, Juno identifies the failed or unavailable source, uses trusted available information when sufficient, and clearly flags uncertainty. If the missing information could materially change the recommendation, Juno pauses and asks for human input rather than guessing.
