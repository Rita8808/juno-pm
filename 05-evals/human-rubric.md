What graders score
What you’re scoring: Junos product risk analysis and prioritization output
Value proposition: Juno helps Product Managers identify important customer risks sooner by analyzing scattered customer feedback, product signals, business priorities, and engineering constraints, then recommending evidence-based next steps.
Dimensions
Dimension	Question	Scale	1 or No	3	5 or Yes
_	Are Juno’s findings supported by the available source information, without invented facts or misleading claims?	Yes / No	Juno invents evidence, misrepresents a source, or makes a material factual error.	n/a	All important claims are supported by the provided signals.
Risk prioritzation	Does Juno prioritize risks appropriately based on customer impact, business consequences, urgency, and product strategy?	1 to 5	Misses a critical risk or prioritizes a minor issue over a major customer or business risk.	Identifies the main risks but misses an important factor or ranks some items incorrectly	Correctly prioritizes the most important risks using the available evidence, business impact, urgency, and strategy.
Calibration
How many answers you’ll score, and how often: Score 10 Juno outputs during the initial evaluation. During the pilot, review 5 outputs each week. Include routine cases, conflicting customer signals, high-impact risks, and cases with missing information.
Who scores them with you: A Product Manager scores each output, with a second reviewer from Product, Engineering, or Customer Success independently scoring the same sample.
What happens when two of you disagree: Review the evidence and scoring definitions together, document the agreed score and rationale, and escalate unresolved disagreements to the designated product owner. Clarify the rubric if the disagreement exposes an ambiguous criterion.
Pass bar
Juno passes when its average score across the three 1-to-5 questions is at least 4.0 out of 5, and both deal-breaker questions receive a Yes. Reviewers must also confirm that Juno does not miss or materially understate a clearly supported critical customer, production, security, or compliance risk.
Fails outright, whatever the average:
An output fails if Juno fabricates evidence, makes a material factual error, takes an unauthorized action, makes an unapproved commitment, or presents a recommendation as certain when critical information is missing. Failed outputs must be reviewed and corrected before they are accepted.
