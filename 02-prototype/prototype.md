## What it demonstrates
A RocketShip PM can open Juno at the start of the day and quickly identify the product and customer risks that may need attention, understand why Juno flagged each risk, and access the supporting evidence from Jira, Slack, and customer feedback, all in one place.


_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The prototype makes the value of Juno easy to understand at a glance. Instead of manually checking multiple sources, the PM gets a focused list of potential risks, the reason each was flagged, and the supporting evidence. Keeping the PM in control also makes the experience feel more like an AI Associate PM than an AI making decisions for the PM.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** The prototype uses sample data and doesn't actually connect to Jira, Slack, or customer systems, so the risk detection is only simulated. 
- **What I'd change next pass:** I would make the underlying evidence interactive so the PM could click a risk and see the actual Jira tickets, Slack conversations, and customer signals that led Juno to flag it. I would also add a way for the PM to provide feedback on Juno's assessment, such as "relevant," "not relevant," or "already being handled," so Juno could become more useful over time.
